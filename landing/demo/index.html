PII Shield

See exactly what the AI would see. This runs 100% in your browser — open devtools and check the network tab: nothing is sent anywhere for this page. Detects Singapore NRIC/FIN, passports, UENs, phone numbers, emails, dates of birth, Luhn-checked card numbers, addresses, postal codes, context-gated bank accounts, and court case numbers.

Engine: regex-only (this demo runs without the spaCy/Presidio NER model the installed app uses, so person and organisation names are not masked here — only the structured identifiers above are).
