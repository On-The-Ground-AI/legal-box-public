WORKPLACE SAFETY AND HEALTH ACT 2006
(EXTRACT — Section 12)

12. Duties of occupiers

(1) It shall be the duty of every occupier of a workplace to take, so far as is reasonably practicable, such measures as are necessary to ensure the safety and health of persons working or lawfully present at the workplace, including —
 (a) providing and maintaining a working environment that is safe and without risk to health;
 (b) providing and maintaining safe means of access to and egress from the workplace;
 (c) ensuring risks are eliminated or minimised through the implementation of appropriate control measures; and
 (d) developing and implementing procedures for dealing with emergencies that may arise while any person is at the workplace.

(2) An occupier who contravenes subsection (1) shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $200,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to both, and, in the case of a continuing offence, to a further fine not exceeding $10,000 for every day or part thereof during which the offence continues after conviction.

RELEVANCE TO CURRENT MATTER
Relevant as the statutory basis for the standard of care owed by an occupier or project manager over a construction site, cited alongside Ng v. Meridian Construction [2025] SGCA 7 in submissions concerning site-safety certification.
