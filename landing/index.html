Self-install guide

Get running in 3 steps

Download the installer, open it, and let the first-launch wizard recommend the right AI model for your hardware. After that one-time setup, the app runs fully offline forever.

1. Download

Click the Download for Mac or Download for Windows button above. The installer comes with the AI runtime built in — nothing else to install.

2. Open it

Mac: Double-click the .dmg file, then drag OTG Legal Box into your Applications folder. First launch may require right-click → Open (one-time Gatekeeper workaround for unsigned builds).

Windows: Double-click the .exe installer and click through. You may see a SmartScreen prompt — click More info → Run anyway.

3. First-launch wizard

The app starts its own AI runtime automatically. A short wizard checks your hardware, recommends the right model size (5–20 GB download), and you're done. After that, the internet is never needed again.

What's inside

A full legal workbench,
zero cloud dependency

Everything a litigation or transactional practice needs, running locally on a Mac Mini, Mac Studio, or any modern laptop.

🔒

PII Shield

Names, NRICs, phone numbers, emails, addresses, and bank accounts are masked before the AI sees any text, then restored in the result. Paste anything into the built-in preview and see exactly what the AI would receive.

🚫

Egress Lock

The app blocks its own network access at the socket level — it cannot reach anything beyond your machine even if it tried. Turn off Wi-Fi and everything keeps working.

💬

Legal Chat

Conversational AI for legal research, statutory interpretation, and procedural questions — every message passes through the PII Shield first.

📄

Contract Review

Upload a contract and receive a structured risk report identifying unusual clauses, missing protections, and key obligations.

📑

Redlining & Word Export

Compare versions or get AI-suggested edits — then export a real Word document with genuine tracked changes, ready for Accept/Reject.

📚

Court Bundles

Auto-sort authorities, generate relevance statements, and download a filing-ready PDF — cover page, table of contents, bookmarks per tab, continuous page numbers.

📅

Chronology

Extract and sort event timelines from multiple litigation documents into an editable chronology.

✍️

Drafting + 1,200-prompt library

First drafts of letters, billing narratives, and pleadings — plus a curated library of over 1,200 legal prompts across nine practice volumes.

🔍

Case Search

Semantic search over your uploaded case library. The index itself stores only masked text — even the search database never holds raw client identities.

📊

Resource Dashboard

Live CPU, memory, and GPU meters show exactly how hard the machine is working — so you always know what the AI is costing you: electricity, nothing more.

📋

Audit Log

Every action is recorded locally with timestamps and PII-masking counts (never the values). Exportable for compliance reviews.

💾

Any document, your choice

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel — with a choice of fast or layout-accurate extraction engines, and optional OCR for scanned files.
